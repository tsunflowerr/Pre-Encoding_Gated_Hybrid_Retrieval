# Hình minh họa truy xuất câu hỏi kỹ thuật

- Công cụ: built-in imagegen, chế độ tạo ảnh mới.
- Tệp dùng trong dự án: `slides/pegh/assets/technical-retrieval.png`.
- Vai trò: minh họa bài toán trên slide 3; không phải dữ liệu hoặc kết quả benchmark.
- Các sơ đồ phương pháp trong `retrieval-methods.svg`, `pegh-architecture.svg`
  và `gate-learning.svg` là SVG có nhãn, dựng theo phương pháp của dự án để có thể sửa.

## Prompt cuối cùng

```text
Use case: scientific-educational.
Asset type: illustration for a Vietnamese university research presentation about technical question retrieval.
Primary request: a clean, precise conceptual illustration showing a new technical question being searched within an existing collection of question documents. A blue question document on the left, a magnifying glass in the center, a small organized archive of white document sheets with blue text-like lines on the right. Two documents in the archive have subtle green matching marks to suggest similar questions. Include a small, understated terminal/code symbol on the new question document to communicate software and Linux questions.
Style/medium: polished flat editorial technical illustration with gentle depth, crisp edges, generous white negative space, suitable for an academic slide. Wide 3:2 composition.
Color palette: white background, UET royal blue #004aad, navy outlines, restrained VNU green #347a1c, very pale blue gray.
Constraints: no people, no robot, no brain, no decorative glowing neural network, no logos, no watermark, no readable words, no numbers or charts, no invented benchmark results. Keep all subjects fully inside the canvas with broad white margins. This illustration explains the retrieval problem, not the algorithm architecture; do not include architecture labels or arrows.
```
